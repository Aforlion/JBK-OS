Specification 036 — Client Delivery & Handover Engine

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved for Implementation

Priority: Critical

Architectural Layer: Client Success & Delivery

Executive Summary

The Client Delivery & Handover Engine manages the final stage of every client engagement.

It transforms completed project deliverables into a structured client handover package, coordinates client acceptance, activates warranty and support, captures organizational knowledge, and formally closes the project.

A project is not complete until the client has accepted the solution.

Mission

Ensure every client engagement ends consistently, professionally, and with complete organizational traceability.

Business Requirements
BR-036-001 Delivery Validation

Before handover, the engine shall verify:

All Work Orders are completed.
All Deliverables are approved.
No critical blockers remain.
Required documentation exists.

If validation fails, handover shall not begin.

# Client Delivery & Handover Architectural Standards

1. A project is not complete until the client formally accepts the delivered solution.

2. Every project must produce a standardized handover package.

3. Warranty and support begin only after client acceptance.

4. Organizational knowledge must be captured before project closure.

5. Executive approval is mandatory before final closure.

6. Project archives must remain permanently traceable.
